Hereglyph — Child safety standards

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Hereglyph prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) absolutely, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any other sexualisation of a minor. There is no exception and no context in which it is tolerated.

These are our published standards, as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

What the app allows people to send

This is the first line of defence, and it is structural rather than a promise.

  • No free text. Every message is assembled from a fixed vocabulary of 2,208 words and 211 sentence patterns, published with the app. A user selects words; they cannot type. Nothing outside that vocabulary can be transmitted.
  • No images, video, audio or files. The app has no camera, gallery, upload, or attachment feature of any kind, so CSAM cannot be sent, stored or shared through it.
  • No private messaging. There are no direct messages, no chat, no follow or friend graph, and no way to contact or search for a specific person. A message is left at a place and read by whoever physically goes there.
  • No profiles. A user has a chosen handle and nothing else — no photo, no biography, no age, no links.

The vocabulary contains no sexual terms. A message that sexualises anybody cannot be composed from it.

Reporting

  • In-app reporting. Every message, and every answer to a message, carries a report action. Reporting also blocks the author immediately: their content stops being shown to the reporter and their locations stop reaching them.
  • Reports reach a person. Reported content and its author are recorded for review, with the reason given.
  • Anyone can report to us directly, without the app, at the contact below.

What we do about a report

  • Content that violates these standards is removed.
  • The account is terminated. Accounts created to evade a termination are terminated.
  • We report apparent CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national authority, and we preserve the associated records as required by law.
  • We comply with lawful requests from law enforcement.

Age

Hereglyph is not directed to children. It is rated for teens and above, is not enrolled in the Google Play Families programme, and its store listing states that it is not intended for children under 13.
