Deleting your Hereglyph data

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Hereglyph is the app for leaving short messages at places, readable only by walking to them. This page covers erasing what you have written and found while keeping your account and your name.

To delete the account itself, see Deleting your account.

How to erase your data

  1. Open Hereglyph.
  2. Tap the gear icon, top right, to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Your data.
  4. Tap Erase beside Erase what you have left and found.
  5. Confirm.

It happens immediately and cannot be undone. Your account stays, your handle stays, and you can carry on using the app straight afterwards with a clean history.

If you have already uninstalled the app, reinstall it and sign in with the same Google account. If you cannot sign in, open an issue at github.com/MrSinisterX/hereglyph-privacy/issues saying what you want erased and the handle you used. Requests are answered within 30 days.

What is erased

  • Every glyph you left, at every place you left one.
  • Every answer you added to someone else's glyph.
  • Everything you found — which glyphs you read, and the coordinate your phone reported when you read each one.
  • Every rating you gave.
  • Every report you made.
  • Crash reports sent by your copy of the app.

What is kept

  • Your account and your name, which is the point of doing this rather than deleting the account.
  • People you have blocked. Blocking is protection rather than history, and quietly undoing it would expose you to somebody you chose not to see again. You can unblock anyone yourself in Settings.
  • Your notification address, so the app can still tell you when somebody reads a glyph you leave next. Turning notifications off in Settings deletes it.
  • Anonymous counts — how many glyphs exist in a city, how often a word gets used. Numbers about places rather than about people, with nothing in them that can be traced back to you.

Taking a copy first

Settings → Your data → Export hands you a file with everything the app holds about you. Do this before step 4, not after.
